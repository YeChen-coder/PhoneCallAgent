# Location and parking
We are at 123 Maple Street, Suite 200, Toronto. Free parking is available behind the building; enter from Oak Lane. The nearest subway stop is King Station, a 5 minute walk.

# Hours
Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 9 AM to 5 PM (closed 12 to 1 for lunch). Wednesday 9 AM to 7 PM. Friday 9 AM to 1 PM. Closed weekends and statutory holidays.

# What to bring
Bring your health card, a list of current medications, and any referral letters. New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to fill in intake forms.

# Cancellation policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Missed appointments without notice may be charged a 40 dollar fee.

# Insurance and payment
Services covered by the provincial health plan are billed directly. Uninsured services such as sick notes, forms, and travel vaccines are paid at the front desk by debit or credit card.

# Prescription refills
Refill requests are handled by the doctor within 2 business days. The agent should take a message with the medication name and pharmacy. Controlled substances require an appointment.

# Test results
Staff cannot give test results over the phone through the automated assistant. The doctor's office will call the patient if follow-up is needed; otherwise results are discussed at the next visit.

# New patients
We are accepting new patients for Dr. Wei Shen (沈医生). Dr. Michael Patel's roster is currently full for new patients but he sees existing patients.

# Languages
Dr. Wei Shen speaks English, Mandarin and Cantonese. Nurse Emily Wong speaks English and Cantonese. The front desk speaks English and Mandarin.

# Walk-ins
We do not accept walk-ins. For urgent same-day needs, call in the morning and we will try to fit you in, or visit the nearest urgent care.

# Emergencies
For chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms, or thoughts of self-harm, hang up and call 911 immediately.
